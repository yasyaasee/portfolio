How it works

From brief to video in 2-3 days

  1. Brief

    You send a product link and packaging photos. We talk about your buyer and what stops them from buying.

  2. Script

    I write 2-3 scripts with the opening hook and a storyboard. You pick one.

  3. Video

    I generate the video with your product and check packaging, text and colors frame by frame.

  4. Revisions

    One free round of edits. You get 9:16 files ready for Reels, TikTok and ads.
