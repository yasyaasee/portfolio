01 · Brand identity

Brand identity, packaging and print

Two brands from scratch: from the meaning of the name and the logo to packaging, dielines, bags and social media visuals.

[amologia]

"I love you, science"

That is what the name means. The goal was to combine strict medicine with living nature: the typography is restrained, almost clinical, while a die-cut window on the box reveals a wave or seaside stones.

seawhitestone
  • Logo in brackets, like a formula
  • Two lines: anti-age and basic care
  • Boxes, dielines, bags, bottles

TRÅD

"Thread" in Danish

This plant-based supplement brand looked like dozens of others: beige stock photos, no product and no people who need it. I started with a content audit and a customer profile - a 35+ person who runs in the morning, reads ingredient lists and pays for quality. That grew into a system: a single orange accent as "life energy", a "thread" line in the infographics, and a feed where every row shows the product, a person and a benefit.

All social media photo content - product shots, people and community - was generated with AI. The jars and labels are real.

05 · Product cards

Covers and slides for marketplaces

I look at a card the way a shopper sees it in search results: what they need to understand in one second and what makes them stop. Everything on the slides is AI-generated and laid out in Figma.

What I changed
  • Before: mascara on an empty background, six same-size captions competing with each other
  • After: the model's lashes in close-up, one headline and two benefits
  • Leaf shadows and warm light make the packaging look premium; the slides work as one series

Funnels: card structure and copy

I write the selling structure of a card: from the ingredients, studies and brand description I pick what matters and lay it out across 10 slides - from the shopper's problem on the cover to contraindications and guarantees. Below are two of my funnels, unedited.
